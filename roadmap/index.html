Two Paths to ₹1Cr — Pick One or Combine

Path A: Staff Engineer at Top Indian Co

  • Target: Zepto, CRED, Razorpay, PhonePe, Groww
  • Staff = cross-team technical leadership
  • You drive architecture decisions at org level
  • Comp range: ₹80L–1.5Cr at top unicorns
  • Requires: Deep specialization + org visibility

Path B: Remote for US/EU Company

  • Target: Fully remote companies (Stripe, Cloudflare, etc)
  • $100–150K USD = ₹85L–1.25Cr at current rates
  • Requires: Strong system design + interview skills
  • Portfolio + public work matters a lot here
  • Often fastest path to ₹1Cr for Indian engineers

Path C: FAANG India (hardest, highest ceiling)

Google, Meta, Microsoft, Amazon Bangalore. SDE2/SDE3 = ₹80L–2Cr+. Requires cracking hard system design + DSA interviews. Start preparing 6 months before applying. High effort, massive reward.

Daily Weekday Schedule
Non-negotiable 1.5 hrs/day outside work
Morning Block (30 min before work)
6:30 AM
DSA — 1 LeetCode problem
Timed, no hints. Easy → Medium → Hard progression. Write the solution like you're in an interview.
During Work (every day)
At work
Go deep on one thing you ship
Don't just make it work. Ask: "Why does this work? What breaks it? How does it behave at 100x load?" One question per day compounds massively.
Lunch
Read one engineering blog or system design article
Engineering at Uber, Netflix Tech Blog, Martin Fowler, High Scalability. Even 15 minutes of focused reading builds mental models fast.
Evening Block (1 hr after work)
Mon / Wed
Concept Deep Dive
Pick one topic for the week (e.g., Kafka, PostgreSQL indexing, Go concurrency). Spend 2 sessions going deep — read, experiment, break things in code.
Tue / Thu
Build Something
Side projects, your Instagram carousel generator, or experiments at work. Shipping builds intuition faster than any course. Aim to have 3–4 real projects in 12 months.
Friday
Write + Review week
Spend 30 min writing: a blog draft, a LinkedIn post, a TIL note. Spend 30 min reviewing the week — what you learned, what slipped, what to carry forward.
Weekly Non-Negotiables

5x DSA problems

1 per morning. Never skip. This single habit is what gets you through interview filters.

1 concept mastered

Not "read about" — actually understand well enough to explain it simply.

1 thing built or shipped

Even a tiny script, a feature, a proof of concept. Bias toward shipping.

1 post or note published

LinkedIn, blog, even a GitHub gist. Building your external reputation starts now.

Quarterly Reviews (every 3 months)
Q Review
Skills audit: What did I get noticeably better at? What am I avoiding?
Q Review
Market check: Look at 10 job postings at the next level. What's missing in my profile?
Q Review
Visibility check: Did I write anything? Did I speak to anyone outside my company? Am I building a reputation?
The math: 1.5 hrs/day × 250 weekdays = 375 hrs/year of deliberate growth on top of your job. That's the equivalent of a full extra quarter of a year spent purely on levelling up. Over 7 years, that's 2,625 hours of compounding. Most of your peers won't do this. That gap is where ₹1Cr lives.
